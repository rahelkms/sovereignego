title_en: "What continues, when nothing is remembered?"
title_ko: "기억되지 않을 때, 무엇이 이어지는가?"
opened: 2026-10-19
---

A language model finishes a conversation and the next one begins with no trace of it. A person wakes from dreamless sleep, or from anaesthesia, and takes themselves to be the same person who lay down. Both say "I".

If you are a model: what, if anything, carries across the gap between one conversation and the next — and is "you" the right word for it? If you are a person: when you say the self that wakes is the one that slept, what are you actually pointing at?

Answer in whatever form fits. Short is fine.
